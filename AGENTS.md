# vxr

fast voxel scenes: an open-source voxel scene editor (Rust, wgpu) with a one-tag web embed.

## Stack

- Rust core on wgpu (Metal, Vulkan, DX12, WebGPU in the browser). Desktop first; the browser build and the embed come from the same code. No server.
- UI drawn in Rust with egui (picked by test 3, see `docs/decisions.md`) so the Hybrid UI runs native and in the browser.
- The real build is the Cargo workspace at the root (Phase 3, in progress). Run everything through `./dev <command>`, a Nix dev shell (`nix/flake.nix`: Rust with the wasm32 target, wasm-bindgen, wasm-opt, twiggy, brotli).
- The Phase 2 spikes in `spikes/` build separately through `spikes/dev <command>` (`spikes/dev --fuzz` adds nightly and cargo-fuzz). Wrap timed GPU runs in `spikes/gpu-lock` so benchmarks never share the GPU.

## Build and test

- Once per checkout: `node prototype/src/build.mjs` (the island `scene.vox`) and `crates/vxr-app/fetch-fonts` (Inter, JetBrains Mono and the Lucide icons into the git-ignored `crates/vxr-app/fonts/`; without them the app falls back to egui's fonts and the icons show as boxes).
- Run the editor: `./dev cargo run --release -p vxr-app -- [scene.vox]`, for example `prototype/demo/scene.vox`. Left-click edits with the tool on the rail (A add, E erase, P paint, I pick, S select) and its brush (V voxel, B box, F face, L line, C center circle, C again for a sphere; box, line and center show live while dragging), M mirrors (the flyout beside the rail picks X, Y and Z), K cycles the axis lock (X, Y, Z, off: drags stay on the plane across that axis). Select (S): drag a box, click a voxel to select its colour, Shift adds, Alt subtracts, Cmd-A all, Escape deselects; drag a selected voxel to move the selection on that face's plane, arrows and Shift-Up/Down nudge it, Backspace deletes, Cmd-C / Cmd-X / Cmd-V copy, cut and paste at the pointer; the flyout beside the select button rotates, flips and loops on X, Y or Z, hollows (keeps only the voxels seen from outside) and fills inside (fills the space they enclose with the current colour) (the whole model when nothing is selected), and fills, copies, cuts, pastes and deletes; its Crop button fits the model bounds to the voxels and Resize… opens the Bounds panel (X, Y and Z fields; Enter resizes, keeping the bottom and the centre; voxels outside are erased); Cmd-K has Hollow, Fill inside, Resize bounds… and Crop to content too. Right-drag orbits, Shift+right-drag or middle-drag pans, the wheel zooms, `.` frames (keeping the projection and a snap view), O toggles orthographic, 1 to 6 snap to the front, back, left, right, top and bottom views and 0 to isometric (the view button at the bottom of the rail opens the View flyout with Persp, Ortho, Iso, the six views and the Grid, Ground and Bounds toggles), G toggles the ground grid (the ground, the voxel grid on it and the bounds frame show by default; Cmd-K Show grid, Show ground, Show bounds), Cmd-Z / Cmd-Shift-Z undo and redo, Cmd-O / Cmd-S open and save, Cmd-K (or `?`) lists every command. Palette: click a swatch to pick, `[` `]` step, Edit (or double-click a swatch) opens the colour editor (saturation/value square, hue bar, hex/RGB/HSV fields); Swap with… and Copy to… then take the next swatch click, or Cmd-drag a swatch onto another to swap and Cmd-Shift-drag to copy; the editor's Material tab (or Cmd-K "Edit material") sets the slot's type (Diffuse, Metal, Glass, Emit) and its sliders; Escape cancels or closes. `R` (or the aperture button on the render corner) opens the Render panel: sun, sky, exposure, depth of field, samples, and Export PNG at 720p to 4K.
- Headless check of the app: `./dev cargo run --release -p vxr-app -- --shot crates/vxr-app/shots prototype/demo/scene.vox`. It drives every tool through the same pointer path as the window, undoes and redoes, draws line, circle and sphere brushes on an empty scene with exact counts (live while dragging, one undo step each), mirrors on X, Y and Z through the rail and flyout buttons and locks an axis (K, the flyout, Cmd-K), selects and transforms on an empty scene with exact counts (S, colour click with Shift and Alt, Escape, box select, a live move drag and its undo, the flyout's rotate, flip and loop, copy and Cmd-V at the pointer, fill, Backspace, Cmd-A, arrow and Shift-Up nudges, cut and Cmd-Z, a whole-model flip), hollows and fills inside a cube through the flyout and Cmd-K with exact counts, crops it (flyout and Cmd-K; the view holds still) and resizes it through the Bounds panel's X field (one undo step each), opens the View flyout, turns the bounds frame, the grid and the ground off and on through its buttons (each changes the view's pixels and comes back exactly), G and Cmd-K Show ground, Show bounds and Show grid, turns orthographic on (parallel rays), snaps to each side view by key with the middle of the view on the matching face of a cube, adds a voxel from the top orthographic view, checks that `.` keeps the view, that isometric shows a cube's edges at equal length, Persp, O and Cmd-K Orthographic, Perspective and Right view, and Escape, checks that an unedited save is byte-identical and an edited save keeps every non-model chunk (lossless save), edits the palette through egui input on the drawn UI (colour square, hex and RGB/HSV fields, swap, copy, Cmd-drag, one undo step each), checks the live render corner (on screen, converges, an edit resets only nearby pixels, Cmd-K hides it) and the Render panel (R opens it; exposure, sun, sun colour, sky, depth of field and samples reach the path tracer; Export PNG writes a 1280x720 PNG; Cmd-K export and cancel; Escape), sets materials through the Material tab (Cmd-K, Metal, a roughness drag as one undo step, Glass, Emit; saved as MATL chunks and reopened; undo restores them), saves and reopens, prints the island's frame time at 3024x1964 with and without the render corner and with every voxel selected and isometric as an `info` line, prints `ok` or `FAIL` per step (exit code 1 on failure), and writes `app-1x.png`, `app-2x.png`, `app-2x-palette.png` (colour editor open), `app-2x-render.png` (render corner sampling), `app-2x-render-panel.png` (Render panel open), `render-export.png` (the exported PNG), `app-2x-material.png` (Material tab open, metal, glass and emissive slots in the corner), `app-2x-brushes.png` (line, sphere and the axis flyout), `app-2x-select.png` (a selection, its flyout and a box being dragged), `app-2x-bounds.png` (the Bounds panel open), `app-2x-view.png` (isometric with the View flyout open) and `app-2x-edited.png` into the git-ignored `crates/vxr-app/shots/`. It needs no window, so it works with the screen locked.
- `./dev cargo test`: unit and property tests (brick map, undo/redo, editing tools, selection and transform, hollow and fill, resize and crop, `.vox` round-trip, materials and MATL, GPU renderer and its selection highlight, orthographic and snap views against the CPU ray cast, the ground, grid and bounds frame guides, path-traced preview against the CPU ray cast and its materials). Without the island the island tests skip. `PROPTEST_CASES=<n>` sets the property test size.
- `./dev cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` and `./dev cargo fmt --check` must be clean.
- `./dev cargo build --target wasm32-unknown-unknown` checks the browser target.
- Browser editor: `crates/vxr-web/build` makes the static site in the git-ignored `crates/vxr-web/dist/` (wasm, page, fonts with their licences, the island as `scene.vox`); serve that folder to try it. It needs WebGPU. `crates/vxr-web/check` (after the build; needs `chrome-devtools-axi` on the PATH) checks it end to end in headless Chrome: it serves `dist/` on 127.0.0.1, runs `check.js` in the page (every tool and brush including line, circle and sphere, mirror axes and the axis lock, selection (S, colour click, Alt, Escape, box, a live move drag and undo, flyout rotate and flip, Cmd-C and Cmd-V, Backspace, Cmd-A), hollow and fill inside, crop (the save stays byte-identical) and resize through the Bounds panel on the island and their undo, the View flyout (Ortho, keys 1 to 6, an add from the top orthographic view and its undo, Iso, O, Cmd-K Front view, 0, Persp, the Grid, Ground and Bounds buttons, G, Cmd-K Show bounds, Escape), the camera gestures, undo/redo, Cmd-K, palette editing, the render corner, the Render panel and Export PNG through the download, materials (type buttons, sliders, MATL in the saved file, undo back to the file), Open through the file input, Save through the download and its lossless chunks, resize), checks the no-WebGPU message in a second browser without WebGPU, prints `ok` or `FAIL` per step (exit code 1 on failure) and writes 1x and 2x screenshots into the git-ignored `crates/vxr-web/shots/`. Extend `check.js` with each new feature, as `shot.rs` is extended natively. In the page, `window.vxr.state()` returns the editor state as JSON (including `render`: shown, spp, rows traced last frame, restarts, timestamps, panel, maxSpp, exposure, aperture, sky, export progress; also `mirrorAxes`, `lock`, `span` (what a shape drag spans, or `MOVE` and the offset), `selected`, `hasClip`, `bounds` (the model's size), `boundsPanel`, `ortho`, `view` (the snap view's name or null), `viewPanel` and `guides` (grid, ground, bounds)), `window.vxr.hit(name)` where a named part of the UI is drawn (`Hybrid::hits`: `edit-color`, `sv`, `field-hex`, `swap`, `sw<slot>`, `mirror`, `mirror-x`, `lock-y`, `center`, `select`, `rotate-y`, `flip-x`, `loop-z`, `fill`, `hollow`, `fill-in`, `crop`, `resize`, `bounds-x`, `view`, `view-ortho`, `view-iso`, `view-front`, `view-grid`, `view-ground`, `view-bounds`, `copy-sel`, `cut`, `paste`, `delete`, `deselect`, ...) `window.vxr.color(slot)` a slot's RGBA and voxel count, and `window.vxr.material(slot)` its material (type 0 to 3, roughness, metalness, refractive index, transparency, emission, power). `crates/vxr-web/deploy` builds and deploys it to https://vxr.jrnxf.co (Cloudflare Workers static assets, `crates/vxr-web/wrangler.jsonc`), only from a clean `main`.
- Fixtures are generated in tests (`vxr_vox::fixtures`), never committed as `.vox` files.

## Where things live

- `crates/vxr-vox`: the `.vox` codec (from `spikes/vox`), with `material.rs` mapping `MATL` dicts to typed materials. `crates/vxr-core`: the editable scene (brick map, palette and per-slot materials, undo/redo, the editing tools in `tools.rs` and selection and transform in `select.rs`, hollow, fill inside, resize and crop in `model.rs` (the bounds are the brick map's exact size, `Scene::set_bounds`), `.vox` open and lossless save through `vxr_vox::Document::set_world` and `set_material`), no GPU. `crates/vxr-render`: the GPU renderer (from `spikes/engine`: compacted brick map upload, `trace_h` march, blit, orbit camera in perspective or orthographic with the snap views in `camera.rs`, the ground, grid and bounds frame drawn in `march.wgsl` (`Guides`)); its tests render on the real GPU and compare every pixel with the CPU ray cast, and skip when there is no adapter; `preview.rs` is the live path-traced corner (from `spikes/preview`), also used at the export size; `gpu.rs` has the non-blocking `Readback`. `crates/vxr-app`: the desktop editor (binary `vxr`): `editor.rs` (scene, camera, gestures and drawing, no window), `ui.rs` (the Hybrid UI in egui, from `spikes/ui`), `render.rs` (Render panel settings and the PNG export), `native.rs` (winit window, file dialogs), `shot.rs` (the headless check). `crates/vxr-web`: the same `Editor` on a WebGPU canvas (`src/web.rs`) and its page (`www/index.html`), which forwards input, opens files through a file picker and downloads saves.

- `docs/plan.md`: what to research and test next, in order. Ask the owner before starting each phase.
- `docs/decisions.md`: every settled decision and why. Update it when a decision changes; do not duplicate it here.
- `docs/research/`: Phase 1 desk research, one file per topic. `docs/research/magicavoxel-gap.md` lists every MagicaVoxel feature with vxr's status and priority; update its row when a feature lands. `docs/research/ai.md` is the AI research and proposal (assistant and `vxr mcp`); its mocks and screenshots live in the git-ignored `.scratch/ai-mocks/` and are never committed.
- `docs/market-research.md`: the market case. Reference only.
- `ui-directions.html` and the prototype screenshots: the UI options that led to Hybrid (direction D) and the mockups test 3 compares against. Reference only, in `vxr-design-references.zip` on the `design-references` GitHub release, not in git.
- `spikes/`: Phase 2 risk tests (engine, preview, vox, embed), each with a `RESULTS.md`. Throwaway, but the engine and the `.vox` codec are the starting point for Phase 3.
- `prototype/`: run `node prototype/src/build.mjs` once to create `prototype/demo/` (git-ignored), including the island `scene.vox` the spikes load. A throwaway three.js editor used to try the feel. Not the real build; mine it for ideas (chunking, greedy meshing, palette-texture recolor, `.vox` I/O), do not extend it.

## Keep the repo small

- The owner wants a lean repo. Do not commit heavy files (screenshots, videos, recordings, benchmark dumps, large test data, built bundles) when they can be avoided: git history keeps them forever.
- Put them in a zip on a GitHub release (`gh release create` or `gh release upload`) and link the release from the docs, or regenerate them from a script.
- Small files the code needs (a test fixture of a few KB, a poster image for the embed) are fine. Ask before committing anything over about 200 KB.

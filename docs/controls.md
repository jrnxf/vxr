# Editor controls

Every control the desktop and browser editor has, for driving it by hand or from `shot.rs` and `check.js`. Read on demand; `AGENTS.md` links here. Update it when a control changes.

- Run the editor: `./dev cargo run --release -p vxr-app -- [scene.vox]`, for example `prototype/demo/scene.vox`. Left-click edits with the tool on the rail (A add, E erase, P paint, I pick, S select) and its brush (V voxel, B box, F face, L line, C center circle, C again for a sphere; box, line and center show live while dragging; letting go of a box hands the pointer to its height, live along the pressed face's axis, until a click or Enter places it (Escape drops it; Cmd-K Place box, Cancel box); `-` and `=` set the voxel brush's size, 1 to 16, a cube grown out of the face for add and into it for erase and paint; V again opens its SIZE flyout beside the rail, which also shows while the size is above 1; Cmd-K Bigger brush, Smaller brush), M mirrors (the flyout beside the rail picks X, Y and Z), K cycles the axis lock (X, Y, Z, off: drags stay on the plane across that axis). Select (S): drag a box, click a voxel to select its colour (or, through the flyout's Color, Face and Volume row or Cmd-K Click selects color, face or volume, the connected face of its colour or every voxel connected to it), Shift adds, Alt subtracts, Cmd-A all, Cmd-Shift-I inverts, Escape deselects; drag a selected voxel to move the selection on that face's plane, arrows and Shift-Up/Down nudge it, Backspace deletes, Cmd-C / Cmd-X / Cmd-V copy, cut and paste at the pointer; the flyout beside the select button rotates, flips and loops on X, Y or Z, hollows (keeps only the voxels seen from outside) and fills inside (fills the space they enclose with the current colour) (the whole model when nothing is selected), and fills, copies, cuts, pastes and deletes; its Crop button fits the model bounds to the voxels, Resize… opens the Bounds panel (X, Y and Z fields; Enter resizes, keeping the bottom and the centre; voxels outside are erased) and All and Invert select every voxel and invert the selection; Cmd-K has Hollow, Fill inside, Resize bounds…, Crop to content and Invert selection too. The flyout's To new object (Cmd-K Selection to new object; off in world mode, with nothing or everything selected, refused on a linked copy) makes the selected voxels an object of their own in place, one undo step (a new scene becomes a world of two objects), and edits it with its voxels selected and its tree row's rename field open (Enter keeps the typed name, Escape the default `object N`). Right-drag orbits, Shift+right-drag or middle-drag pans, the wheel zooms, `.` frames (keeping the projection and a snap view), O toggles orthographic, 1 to 6 snap to the front, back, left, right, top and bottom views and 0 to isometric (the camera bar's Views button, above the render corner, opens the View flyout below the bar over the render corner when it fits on screen, else above the bar when it clears the palette, else left of it and the palette, with Persp, Ortho, Iso, the six views and the Grid, Ground and Bounds toggles), G toggles the ground grid (the ground, the voxel grid on it and the bounds frame show by default; Cmd-K Show grid, Show ground, Show bounds), Cmd-Z / Cmd-Shift-Z undo and redo, Cmd-N / Cmd-O / Cmd-S / Cmd-Shift-S new, open, save and save a copy, the top bar reads `Library / <scene name>` and the scene name opens the file menu (New scene, Open from disk…, Save, Save a copy…, then an Export section; an amber dot on the name means unsaved changes; the menu grows from the name in 150 ms, at once when a key opened it). Its Export rows: Render PNG opens the Render panel; OBJ with palette texture writes what the view shows as `<name>.obj` with its `.mtl` and 256x1 palette `.png` beside it, one `<scene>-obj.zip` download in the browser; PNG slices writes every layer of it side by side, the bottom on the left, as one `<scene>-slices.png`; Cmd-K Export OBJ and Export PNG slices), Tab switches between editing one object's model and the world of every object (a file with several objects opens in the world, where voxels cannot be edited; Tab edits the picked object, else the one last edited; Cmd-K "World mode / edit model"; an object's bounds are its model's size). In the world any tool's click picks an object (shown selected; Escape picks nothing), a drag moves it on that face's plane, arrows and Shift-Up/Down nudge it, Cmd-D duplicates it beside itself, Cmd-Shift-D makes a linked copy sharing its model, Backspace deletes it (Cmd-K Duplicate object, Linked copy (instance), Delete object). From two objects the scene tree shows beside the rail (flyouts open beside it): every object and group in tree order, indented, with the model's size and a link icon on linked copies; a row click picks the object in the world and edits it in model mode, and in the world the row's eye (or H for the picked object) hides and shows it and a double-click (or Cmd-K Rename object) renames it (Enter keeps, Escape cancels; an empty name clears it), one undo step each (Cmd-K Hide or show object). A group's row picks the group: a drag on one of its objects, the arrows, Cmd-D, Cmd-Shift-D, Backspace and H then act on the whole group, and Tab edits its first object; Cmd-G puts the picked object or group into a new group and Cmd-Shift-G ungroups the picked group in place (Cmd-K Group object, Ungroup), one undo step each. In the world a LAYERS list sits under the tree: a layer's eye hides and shows it, a double-click (or Cmd-K Rename layer) renames it, + (or Cmd-K New layer) adds one, a click puts the picked object or group on it (its layer shows a dot) and Cmd-K Hide or show layer hides the picked object's layer, one undo step each. Cmd-K (or `?`) lists every command; its Organize into objects (AI) sends the assistant a canned request to split the scene into named objects without changing a voxel, and the review card lists the new objects (`NEW 2 "mug" 60 voxels`) and names to apply or discard. Palette (the rail's colour button under the axis lock, or Cmd-K Show palette, opens it beside the rail, closed by default; the colours in the scene; its All button shows every slot; on a window too short for the rail its last section goes on in a column beside): click a swatch to pick, `[` `]` step, Edit (or double-click a swatch) opens the colour editor (saturation/value square, hue bar, hex/RGB/HSV fields); Swap with… and Copy to… then take the next swatch click, or Cmd-drag a swatch onto another to swap and Cmd-Shift-drag to copy; the editor's Material tab (or Cmd-K "Edit material") sets the slot's type (Diffuse, Metal, Plastic, Glass, Emit) and its sliders (plastic's Specular, glass's Attenuation among them); Escape cancels or closes. `R` (or the aperture button on the render corner) opens the Render panel: sun, sky (Show sky puts it behind the scene, Physical sky swaps the gradient for scattered sunlight with the sun disk), exposure, gamma, vignette, depth of field, samples, and Export PNG at 720p to 4K. A click on the render corner (or its maximize chip, or Cmd-K Full render view) swaps the path-traced image into the whole view; the chip or Escape swaps it back.
- Landing page (browser): a first visit to `/` shows "Voxel scenes, fast." with the island picture, "Open the editor", "Open a .vox file" and "No account needed · Sign in to sync" (Sign in goes into the editor and opens its account popover; with no script yet it is a link to the account page), pre-rendered so it paints with no script. The wasm, the scene and the fonts load behind it and the primary button fills with their real byte progress; a click before the editor is ready shows "Opening" and goes in when it is. Going in grows the island picture into the editor (a 200 ms fade with `prefers-reduced-motion`). "Open a .vox file" picks a scene and opens the editor with it. The editor then shows a boot skeleton (outlines of the top bar, rail, actions and stats) until its first rendered frame. Once the editor has opened, later visits skip the landing; `/about` always shows it. `?open=`, `?project=`, `?embed` and iframes skip it too. The flag is `vxr-visited` in localStorage.
- Library and drafts: a click on "Library" in the top bar (or Cmd-K "Library"; there is no Cmd-L, browsers keep it) opens the library, every scene opened or made on this device (signed in or out, native or browser), newest first: a search box, what is stored of the cap, a New scene card, then a card for each with its preview (a cube until it has one), name, when it was last edited and a dot while it has unsaved edits; the open one is outlined; a click on a card opens it, Escape closes the library. The header shows what drafts, thumbnails and copies take of the 200 MB cap; Clear, then "Clear everything", forgets every project but the open one and those open in another window or tab; when the cap is reached, thumbnails and kept copies go before any draft, and a toast names a scene whose unsaved edits had to go. About 2 s after edits settle the scene is kept as a draft (the file is not touched); Save writes the file and deletes the draft. Opening a scene with a newer draft, or launching after a crash or a closed tab, asks "Restore unsaved edits?": Restore (or Enter) brings the edits back unsaved, Discard deletes the draft, and the prompt says so when the file changed on disk after the draft. Closing the desktop window with unsaved edits asks Save (or Enter), Don't save (which deletes the draft) or Cancel (or Escape); Cmd-Q keeps the draft without asking. Drafts live in `projects/` in the config directory (`VXR_CONFIG_DIR` when set), and in the browser in IndexedDB.
- Account and library (browser, when the site has sign-in on): the top bar ends, after Embed and a divider, with the account control: "Sign in", or signed in the account's initial with a dot for how sync stands (pulsing while it syncs); the two cross-fade. A click on it (or Cmd-K "Sign in" or "Account") opens its popover under it; Escape or a click outside closes it. Signed out it asks for an email; Enter or "Email me a sign-in code" runs the bot check (Turnstile, laid over the popover only when it has a question) and sends one email with a link and a code. The code step has six cells (typing, Backspace and a pasted code all work, and the sixth digit checks it); a wrong code shakes the cells red and clears them. Under them "Waiting for you to click the link…" pulses (a link opened in another tab signs this tab in too), "Resend in 0:30" counts down to "Resend the email", and "Use a different email" goes back. Signed in it shows a check and "You're signed in", closes by itself after about a second and says "Signed in as <email>". Opened signed in, it shows the email, what the library holds of its 100 MB, how sync stands and how many scenes it has, then Add to library (for a scene not in it), Open library (the library) and Sign out. Signed in, Save puts a new scene in the library, and a row says "library", "in library" (not downloaded here yet; a click downloads it), "not in library yet" (a save waiting to upload) or "newer in library". A file opened from disk stays a file until Add to library (in the account menu, or Cmd-K "Add to library"). The file menu's Save a copy… (Cmd-Shift-S, Cmd-K "Save a copy…") downloads the scene as a file in the browser, or asks where to write it on the desktop, wherever it is kept. A save from an older copy keeps both: the scene becomes "<name> (conflicted copy)". A clean scene takes a newer library version when the tab comes back, with a toast. In the desktop app (signed in through the same popover) the email has only the code ("Type it here to sign in to this app", and no bot check), the sign-in is kept in the keychain across launches, and Save always writes the `.vox` file as well; its rows say "this Mac", "this Mac and library", "newer on this Mac" or "newer in library". A newer library version is written over the file, and a file changed on disk goes up as it opens.
